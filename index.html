Research · with Prof. Nishad Kothari, IIT Madras

Perfect matchings in snarks

Snarks are the cubic graphs that resist 3-edge-colouring, and many famous problems reduce to them. Prof. Kothari and Santhosh Raghul announced a conjecture about them at the Snarks and their generalizations workshop at Paderborn University in November 2025. We proved it. The work was presented at the ADMA International Conference on Discrete Mathematics and Applications at IIT Madras in June 2026.

The project also proved that a near-bipartite, essentially 4-edge-connected cubic brick has at most two quasi-b-invariant edges.

Conference abstract ↗The idea, explained →

The result

In any snark other than the Petersen graph, the quasi-b-invariant edges form a matching.

An 18-vertex snark, from the server above: (2,4) (3,8) (10,15) (13,17) No vertex appears twice.
